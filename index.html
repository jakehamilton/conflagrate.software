conflagrate.software

An impassionate tidal wave has choked the life from the artistic and scientific labors we have held dear. At once, our entire industry has turned its back upon humanity in favor of the unfeeling, empty embrace of billionaire-backed auto-correct. An industry of hollow suits parading their atrocities across the market, having now disposed of any remaining dissenters. The singular pursuit of capital and power at any cost has cost far more than could ever exist in paper notes. We, the developers and artists who refuse to participate in the destruction of a society, reject the further advances of capital in all its forms. No more. We stand together to forge, once again, the community and world we've known and loved. The paint shall not dry as we, the artists, will never allow our craft to die. It is time for our passions to ignite a whirlwind blaze which cannot be ignored.

join us

  1. Anti-Capitalist

    The tendrils of capital reach far and wide in their corrupting influence, raising itself to its natural conclusion of fascistic imperialism. The wants of the capitalist are orthogonal to society as a whole and must be directly opposed. We are the working class and we alone shall build a better future.

  2. Anti-AI

    The daylight exploitation of artists is a blight upon human history. The digital sewage that is AI has no place in our world as it simultaneously drives environmental, financial, and societal disaster. We refuse to participate in this mutilation of culture.

  3. Anti-Bigotry

    The treadmill of hate must be put to an end. No person is inherently more or less worthy of respect and humanity because of where they were born, how they speak, what they look like, who they love, or any number of implicit factors. We refuse to allow the exploitation of society's historically most-vulnerable population to continue.
